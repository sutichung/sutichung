# ZENTRAX Brand System

**Brand:** ZENTRAX  
**Positioning:** Next-generation financial technology  
**Visual direction:** Black / metallic gold  
**Token:** ANT (symbol remains ANT)  
**Repository branch:** `ant-token-testnet`

## 1. Brand architecture

- **ZENTRAX** is the platform and product-family brand.
- **ANT** remains the token name and ticker. Do not rename the token contract, ticker, deployed addresses, or token metadata as part of this visual rebrand.
- Product copy may use **ZENTRAX ANT** when the platform-token relationship needs clarification.
- The existing Ant Colony module may retain its functional identity, presented as an ecosystem module within ZENTRAX.

## 2. Positioning and voice

**Tagline:** NEXT-GEN FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY

ZENTRAX brings market intelligence, quantitative research, AI-assisted analysis, risk monitoring, and blockchain tools into one technology platform.

Voice: precise, confident, technically credible, transparent, and calm. Avoid claims of guaranteed returns, risk-free trading, guaranteed liquidity, exchange listings, or verified capabilities that are not deployed.

## 3. Visual identity

- Primary background: near-black `#080808` / `#0B0B0D`.
- Primary metallic gold: `#D6B56D`.
- Highlight gold: `#F2D99A`.
- Muted gold: `#8D7442`.
- Primary text: `#F5F1E8`.
- Secondary text: `#B8B1A2`.
- Borders: `rgba(214,181,109,0.24)`.
- Use restrained gold highlights, fine metallic borders, geometric forms, generous negative space, and accessible text contrast.
- Avoid excessive glow, fake chrome textures, and gold-colored body text at small sizes.

## 4. Product naming

- Platform: ZENTRAX
- AI and quantitative research: ZENTRAX Intelligence
- Market workspace: ZENTRAX Markets
- Crypto research: ZENTRAX Crypto
- Blockchain ecosystem: ZENTRAX ANT
- Token: ANT

These are brand labels only; they do not imply that each product module is independently deployed or commercially available.

## 5. Token continuity and safety

This rebrand is a presentation and documentation change only. It does not change:
- ANT token symbol, supply, decimals, burn behavior, or contract permissions.
- Sale-contract fee logic (currently specified as 5% of gross USDT).
- Wallet addresses, multisig signers, oracle configuration, deployment network, or contract bytecode.
- Any existing member balances or transaction history.

Never publish an unverified contract address, claim a mainnet launch, or suggest that a testnet token has monetary value. Any future token metadata or contract change requires a separate technical review and explicit approval.

## 6. Brand and legal checks before public launch

- Check trademark conflicts in Taiwan and each intended market.
- Check domain availability and social-handle availability; no availability is asserted by this document.
- Confirm ownership/licensing of fonts, icons, illustrations, and other brand assets.
- Publish one canonical official-links page and anti-impersonation guidance.
- Ensure product descriptions match actual deployment and data-source status.

## 7. Implementation scope

This repository rebrand updates app title/metadata, visible platform header, social preview artwork, README and project documentation. It does not constitute a production deployment. Review responsive layouts, browser metadata previews, accessibility contrast, and build/test results before merging or release.
